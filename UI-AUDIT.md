# DataDeal interface audit

November 25, 2026. The audit covered the local site at 1440 px and 390 px. The guest route walkthrough included existing reports, a missing report, an empty and a populated comparison, and the account routes before sign-in. The Google sample and score history used separate browser sessions.

They now share rows with the domain on the left, the score on the right, and one link across the entire row. Search still opens the requested domain. ## Signed-in follow-up

Chromium checked the signed-in account overview, active Pro status, developer tools, a populated watchlist, and alert preferences at both widths. The developer token buttons and watchlist form now have space around their controls. The test used a local mail outbox and a seeded Pro subscription. Live billing and the populated alert timeline were outside this visual check.

## Rewarded test

On the normal local BILD report, Chromium allowed advertising, requested Google’s official sample, opened it, completed it and closed it. The local server granted access and the same browser could reload the unlocked report. Public rewarded inventory and verified settlement still require the live Google configuration.
